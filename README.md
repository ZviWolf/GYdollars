**$GY Counter** — the Gan Yisroel dollar tracker for CGI Toronto 5786.

Counselors keep track of how many $GY dollars each camper in their bunk has
earned. Open it in any browser, phone or laptop — there's nothing to install.

## Counselors

Sign up with your email, a password, and your bunk number. You'll get a
verification email; click the link and you're in. Each bunk number belongs to
one account, so pick yours carefully.

Once you're in:

- **Add a camper** with the ➕ button
- **Give or take dollars** — tap `+` or `−` next to a camper, then choose 1, 5,
  10, or 20
- **Remove a camper** by tapping ✕ and typing their name to confirm

Everything saves on its own. The line under the title tells you where things
stand — it'll say *saved* after your changes go through.

Forgot your password? There's a reset link on the login screen.

## Head counselors

Log in with the username `headcounselor` and the shared password. You'll see
every bunk in camp and can tap into any of them to see that bunk's campers and
their totals.

## Admins

Log in with the username `admin`. You get the same camp-wide view as a head
counselor, plus the ability to fix things:

- Adjust any camper's dollars in any bunk
- Remove a camper from any bunk
- Delete a whole bunk, which clears its campers and frees up that bunk number
  for someone else

Deleting anything asks you to type the name or bunk number first, so it's hard
to do by accident.
